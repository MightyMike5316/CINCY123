## Brand

`westside old window repair` has no measurable volume yet (new business). The exact un-hyphenated spelling appears in the home title, hero lead, footer and schema `alternateName` so the site ranks first the moment anyone searches it. The hyphenated display name stays in the header.

## What was deliberately NOT targeted

- `window repair near me` (74,000/mo) — only winnable through the Business Profile / map pack, not page copy.
- `window repair service` / `window repair company` (1,900 each) — dominated by national chains; not worth diluting the page.
- `stained glass window repair cincinnati` (390) — Mark doesn't do stained glass.
- Anything glass-replacement, vinyl, auto or commercial.

## How to re-check

Semrush → Keyword Overview → paste the primary list above (semicolon-separated). If a term's volume or KD moves a lot, or a new term shows up in Search Console's *Performance → Queries*, move it into the right page's H2/FAQ rather than stuffing it into existing sentences.

## Note on `<meta name="keywords">`

Each page carries a `keywords` meta tag listing its targets. Google ignores this tag for ranking (since 2009); it's included because it costs nothing, some smaller engines and AI crawlers still read it, and it documents each page's intent in the source. The ranking work is done by the titles, H1/H2s, body copy, FAQ schema and internal links.
