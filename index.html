Repair, not replacement

Why repair your old windows instead of replacing them?

The wood windows in Westwood, West Price Hill, Covedale and Delhi homes were built from old-growth lumber and designed to be serviced. Most "worn out" windows just need the right repair.

They were built to last a century

Every part of a vintage double-hung window — cords, weights, pulleys, glazing, hardware — can be repaired or replaced. A vinyl replacement cannot, and typically lasts 15–25 years before it's landfill.

Repair costs a fraction of replacement

A re-roped, re-glazed and weather-tightened original window costs far less than a new unit and installation, and you keep the trim, the glass and the look of your home. See the full repair vs. replacement comparison.

You keep your home's character

Wavy glass, true divided lights and deep sills are part of what makes an older Cincinnati home worth owning. Mark fixes the real problem and leaves the character intact. Read Mark's approach.

Sound familiar?

Window won't stay open? Painted shut? Signs your old windows need Mark

  • The lower sash won't stay open — you prop it with a stick or a book.
  • A window is painted or swollen shut and hasn't opened in years.
  • You can hear a frayed or broken cord, or a weight banging inside the wall.
  • Glazing putty is cracked, crumbling or missing and the glass rattles in the wind.
  • Drafts and dust come through the sash even when it's locked.
  • A casement window grinds on the crank or won't close tight.
  • A storm panel is loose, cracked or no longer fits its frame.
